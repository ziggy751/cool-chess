A poll result measures participation in that poll. A view count is a reported snapshot. Recent entries have dated coverage. ## Music and adaptation

Jersey-club training edits use quick repeated kicks. Brazilian funk edits leave rhythmic holes around vocal cues. Drift phonk emphasizes distorted bass and a metallic lead. Aura edits often drop the beat just before the gesture. The songs in AI Music Lab use original melody, chord, drum and formant-vocal arrangements with sections and transitions. The serious object-opera arrangement holds its notes while the more rhythmic genres cut on short syllables. The historical trend sources explain the editing practice.
