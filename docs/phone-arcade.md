# Side Reel phone and arcade

In a match, select **Open Side Reel phone** below the board or press **P**. Pick an app from **Home**. Use the bottom navigation to return Home or reopen a recent app. On a wide display, choose the size and side under **Settings**. On a narrow display, the phone sits below the board so every square stays available.

The phone saves its wallpaper, finish, brightness, app order and other preferences in this browser. **Arrange** lets you reorder apps, group them into folders and change the dock. The lock screen can be used on demand or on reopen. Locking, minimizing and closing pause media. Enable background music to keep listening while using another app. The mini player gives you a pause control. A separate switch keeps music playing when the phone closes. Switch on **Reduce motion** to disable decorative transitions.

| App | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| YouTube Shorts | Watch a curated, shuffleable feed in the phone using YouTube’s embedded player. Swipe the video grip or use the arrow controls. |
| AI Shorts | Browse pre-generated footage or screen batches of three original animated Brainrot memes. Use format filters, history, bookmarks, narration and effects controls. |
| Arcade | Browse 23 entries, including Chess Puzzles, with screenshots captured from the games. Find titles or categories, see personal bests, and add favourites or phone Home shortcuts. |
| Flappy Wings | Tap, click, or use Space to flap through the gates. Reach eight points to unlock the kingfisher skin. Pick from six environments. |
| Snake | Play with arrow keys, a swipe, or the direction pad. Change board size, theme, skin, or obstacle mode. |
| 2048 | Slide with arrows, swipes, or the onscreen direction pad. The board and score resume when reopened. |
| Geometry Runner | Tap, click, or use Space to jump. Choose a difficulty, a player shape and a theme. Practice checkpoints leave the personal best alone. |
| Stack Tower | Tap, click, or use Space to drop blocks as they line up. Build a combo by placing consecutive blocks accurately. |
| Mini Racing | Steer with the arrow keys, a horizontal swipe, or the steering buttons. Race two opponents or run a three-lap time trial on city, snow, desert and grassland circuits. Each track keeps its own personal lap record. |
| Block Puzzle | Drag a shape onto the grid, or select it and tap a square. Fill a row or column to clear it. Your board, tray and score resume when reopened. |
| Chess Puzzles | Train on eight positions with starter and challenge filters. The phone board shows the explanation after solving and saves your progress. |
| Music | Play five licensed Kevin MacLeod recordings, the 14 original Side Reel Studio tracks and Winter Bells. Use seeking, shuffle, repeat, favourites and playlists. You can also import a permitted local audio file for this session outside competitive play. |
| AI Music Lab | Generate an original comedy song in a browser worker. Save a seed or favourite and replay the result with synchronized lyrics. |
| Open Spotify | Open the official Spotify website and save official playlists for quick access. |
| Settings | Change the frame, case, wallpaper, placement, display and sound. Change automatic pausing and accessibility options. |
| Side Reel | Play the original built-in clips. Local or casual AI play also supports local MP4 and WebM files from the current browser session. |

The chess clock keeps running on your turn. The phone displays a turn notice, and its default settings pause current videos and games when the turn changes. Resume from the app’s controls. Competitive matches disable YouTube and Chess Puzzles and limit the native clip library to approved, non-instructional videos.

Closing, minimizing, or changing apps pauses the Shorts player. Reopen the app to return to the current Short. [YouTube Shorts setup and testing](youtube-shorts.md)
